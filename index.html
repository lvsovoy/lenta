Built for Power & Simplicity

Everything you need to view, browse, and organize your files across devices and cloud drives.

Universal Media Viewer

Stream and play video (MKV, MP4, WebM) with hardware acceleration, audio with live waveform preview, comics (CBZ/CBR), documents (PDF/EPUB), and animated GIFs.

☁

Multi-Cloud Storage

Directly connect Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, and Nextcloud alongside your local storage. Seamlessly browse remote folders and stream without downloading everything.

🔒

Privacy-First Architecture

Client-only processing with zero analytics, no telemetry, and no intermediate servers. OAuth tokens and files stay strictly on your local device.

🎨

Material 3 Dynamic Colors

Beautiful, adaptive UI that pulls wallpaper accent colors on Android 12+ and seamlessly supports light and dark system themes.

⚡

Fast Smart Thumbnails

Smart video frame selection and background thumbnail caching generate crystal-clear previews while optimizing device RAM and storage.

🔄

Seamless Updates

Built-in update checker alerts you to new releases with rich changelogs and one-tap APK installation directly from GitHub.

Legal & Privacy Transparency
