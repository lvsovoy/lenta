Privacy Policy

Application: Lenta for Android Effective Date: September 9, 2026 License: Open Source (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)

1. Introduction & Core Privacy Commitment

Lenta ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") is an open-source media viewer and multi-cloud storage browser for Android devices. We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Lenta is built with an absolute privacy-first, client-only architecture.

Lenta operates entirely on your device. We do not operate proxy servers, analytics databases, tracking infrastructure, or cloud relay services. Your data flows directly and exclusively between your Android device and the storage providers you choose to connect.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • No Telemetry or Usage Tracking: The app contains no analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics) and does not track screen views or interactions.
  • No Advertising or Marketing Identifiers: Lenta is 100% ad-free and does not read or transmit the Android Advertising ID (AAID).
  • No File Metadata Tracking: The names, paths, sizes, and contents of your local and cloud files are processed solely on-device.

3. Cloud Storage & Third-Party Services

Lenta allows you to optionally connect third-party storage providers. When you configure these services, Lenta interacts directly with their official APIs:

Google Drive API

When you connect Google Drive, Lenta uses Google OAuth 2.0 to obtain temporary access tokens directly on your device. Tokens are used strictly to list folders, download thumbnails, and stream media files requested by you.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance: Lenta's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Microsoft OneDrive (Microsoft Graph API)

When you connect Microsoft OneDrive, authentication is performed via Microsoft Entra ID (OAuth 2.0). Tokens are stored locally in Android EncryptedSharedPreferences/Keystore and used solely to communicate with Microsoft Graph API endpoints (graph.microsoft.com) to access user-selected files.

Nextcloud & ownCloud

Connections use standard WebDAV over HTTPS. Server credentials and app passwords are stored exclusively on your device.

Network Shares (SMB, WebDAV, FTP)

Credentials for local network shares (SMB/CIFS, generic WebDAV, and FTP servers) remain strictly within the app's private sandbox and are never transmitted outside your configured network targets.

4. Android Permissions & On-Device Storage

Lenta requests only permissions strictly necessary for core functionality:

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to communicate directly with your configured cloud and network storage providers, and to check for GitHub release updates.
  • Read Media / Storage (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Required to browse and play media stored on your local device or SD card.
  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Optional permission used solely for scanning QR codes when quickly connecting Nextcloud or network accounts. No image or video footage is ever stored or transmitted.

5. Data Security & Local Encryption

All sensitive authentication tokens and server configuration details are stored in the application's private, sandboxed Android storage. Lenta utilizes standard Android platform security mechanisms to prevent unauthorized access by other applications.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Because Lenta is open source, any modifications to network communication or permission handling will be documented in version commit logs and release notes. Any updates to this policy will be published at this URL.
